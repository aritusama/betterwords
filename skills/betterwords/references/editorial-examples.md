# Editorial examples

These invented, self-contained examples illustrate decisions under the rules. They add no requirements and prescribe no universal wording. Read the complete rule file before applying it; use these examples when a similar editorial decision needs clarification.

## Preserve the author's effective phrasing

Request: Copyedit my memoir excerpt for grammar and punctuation. Keep my voice.

Author's draft:

> I kept the bent key. It opened nothing, but throwing it away felt like moving out twice.

Editorial decision: Leave it unchanged. The supplied first-person perspective belongs to the author. The metaphor carries the passage's meaning, and the sentence is grammatical. Rules 3.5, 6.2, and 8.3 permit this phrasing. Replacing it with a flat description would erase the reason for the passage.

## Paraphrase a source independently

Request: Write one factual sentence from these notes for a repair log. Do not quote the source.

Source note:

> Every cold start was another roll of the dice: the laptop booted on three of five attempts.

One suitable result:

> The laptop booted successfully in three of five cold-start attempts.

Editorial decision: Preserve the observed result and test scope. The source's metaphor is unnecessary in the repair log. Rule 8.3 calls for independently phrased source-based prose here. Do not infer a cause, a long-term failure rate, or a repair recommendation from five attempts.

## Keep required format and precise terminology

Request: Copyedit this methods slide. Keep the heading and bullets. The source confirms the method and limitation.

Slide and source:

```text
Overview
- Dynamic panel model with robust standard errors
- Early results; causal effect remains uncertain
```

Editorial decision: Leave it unchanged. The required heading and slide fragments fit the destination. The words "dynamic" and "robust" are precise methods terminology under 3.10 and 4.1. Removing them because they also appear in a vocabulary warning would damage the meaning.

## Stop after a clean copyedit

Request: Copyedit this Ukrainian update without changing its tone or facts.

Draft and source:

> У вівторок ми додали збережені фільтри до експорту. Тепер потрібний набір можна вибрати зі списку.

Editorial decision: Return the text unchanged. It is clear, grammatical, and supported by the supplied source. A second copyedit should also leave it alone unless a reviewer can name a remaining defect. Rules 7.1, 7.2, and 8.3 support natural Ukrainian and proportionate editing; they do not require visible intervention.
